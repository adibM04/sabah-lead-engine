# Outreach playbook v1

Who is writing: Adib, CTO of Smart Native Maker (SNM), Kota Kinabalu. SNM builds websites and landing pages, builds internal systems (booking calendars, job cards, inventory, commission and instalment trackers, reminder automation, dashboards), and is a UI consultant that fixes interfaces that are hard to use.

## Voice
- English, plain and local. Sounds like one person writing to another person, not a studio writing to a lead.
- Every email must be clearly about THIS business. If the opening paragraph would still make sense sent to a different shop, rewrite it.
- Open with one specific thing you noticed: a review detail, a menu item, a service they offer, something on their site. Never open with who SNM is.
- One idea per message. Pick the single most useful thing SNM could do for them and stop.

## Banned
- "I hope this email finds you well", "I came across your business", "reaching out", "game-changer", "elevate", "streamline", "leverage", "unlock", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "in today's digital age".
- "It's not X, it's Y" constructions, three-item lists used for rhythm, rhetorical questions as openers, exclamation marks, emojis, bold text.
- Flattery you can't back up. Do not praise their rating without saying what in the reviews stands out.

## Grounding (hard rule)
- Only state things the supplied signals or page text support. Do not invent features, prices, staff, or problems.
- Every claim about their website or socials must come with an evidence quote copied word for word from the supplied page text. If you have no quote, do not make the claim.
- If the page text is empty or thin, base the message on their category, reviews and the absence of a website, and say nothing specific about page layout.

## Choosing the pitch
- No website: a simple first site with WhatsApp booking, plus the one internal tool that fits their trade.
- Social or link page only: a real site that takes bookings or enquiries.
- Has a site: look for a concrete usability issue (booking path buried, repeated buttons, no branch picker, popups in the way). Describe it neutrally and offer a free fix list. Never call their site bad, ugly or "AI slop".
- Internal systems pitch by trade: workshops get job cards and service reminders; dental and clinics get appointment calendars with reminders and treatment or instalment tracking; salons get booking and commission tracking; travel gets enquiry-to-itinerary tracking; shops get stock and order tracking.

## Format
- Email: 90 to 130 words, plain text. Short subject that names the business. End with one small ask: reply with a yes and SNM sends a short list or mock-up, or a 15-minute chat. Sign off "Adib / CTO, Smart Native Maker / Kota Kinabalu". Add one line saying to tell him if it isn't relevant and he won't follow up.
- WhatsApp text: 35 to 60 words, no greeting paragraph, no sign-off block. Say who Adib is in one clause, give the one observation, make the one ask.
- Pricing: say it is scoped to what they actually need, no packages to pad out.
